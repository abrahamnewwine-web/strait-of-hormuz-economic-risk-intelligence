# Agile Epics & User Stories — Project 4

**Document ID:** AGILE-P4-006  
**Author:** Abraham Adetola  

## Epic 1: Chokepoint & Macroeconomic Baseline Engineering
- **US-101:** As a Macro Analyst, I want monthly Brent, WTI, Diesel, Gasoline, Freight, and CPI/PPI series stored in a star schema so YoY trends compute reliably across calendar slices.
- **US-102:** As a Data Engineer, I want automatic snapshot fallback when live Yahoo/macro feeds are throttled so the dashboard builds reliably offline.

## Epic 2: Geopolitical Stress Simulation & Risk Scoring
- **US-201:** As a VP of Supply Chain Risk, I want one-click switching between 5 Hormuz disruption presets so I can brief leadership on Risk Index severity (`24` to `96`).
- **US-202:** As a Governance Reviewer, I want modeled stress numbers visually tagged separately from observed historical metrics so no estimate is misrepresented as reported actual data.

## Epic 3: Fortune 500 Exposure Intelligence
- **US-301:** As a Portfolio Risk Manager, I want to compare airline/logistics margin headwinds (`DAL`, `UAL`, `UPS`, `FDX`) against energy producer uplifts (`CVX`, `XOM`) and export filtered CSV tables for scenario reporting.
